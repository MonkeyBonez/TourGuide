# Data and credits

Tour Guide fetches everything it shows at the time you plan a walk. None of it is ours.

- **Streets and places**: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database
  License (ODbL 1.0) — https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. Fetched through public Overpass
  API instances (maps.mail.ru, overpass-api.de, overpass.private.coffee, overpass.kumi.systems).
- **Fame and facts**: Wikidata, CC0 — https://www.wikidata.org.
- **Card text**: Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0 — each card links to its article.
- **Card photos**: Wikimedia Commons; each photo's author and licence are on its Commons page,
  linked from the card.
- **Map display and place cards**: Apple Maps (MapKit).
- **Routing**: our own corridor router (the Like Water engine, `Engine/`), not Apple's or Google's.
- **Translation**: Apple's on-device Translation framework; translated text is marked.
